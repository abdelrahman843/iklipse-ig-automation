Privacy Policy

Iklipse · Last updated 7 October 2026

Iklipse is a private tool that answers comments and direct messages on our own Instagram professional account. It is not offered to other businesses. This policy explains what information it handles when you comment on our posts or message our account, and what we do with it.

What we receive

When you comment on one of our posts or send us a message, Instagram (Meta) shares with us:

  • your Instagram-scoped ID, username, name and profile picture;
  • the text of your comment or message, and the buttons you tap in our replies;
  • whether you follow our account;
  • anything you choose to type in reply to our questions, such as an email address or phone number.

How we use it

  • to send you the reply or information you asked for;
  • to remember where you are in a conversation so we don't repeat ourselves;
  • to let a member of our team read and answer your messages;
  • to count, in aggregate, how well our replies work.

We only message you in reply to something you did, within the time limits Instagram sets. You can stop automated messages at any time by replying STOP.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone for advertising.
  • We don't use it for anything other than talking with you on Instagram.
  • We don't buy or import lists of people to message.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored in our database hosted by Supabase, and is reachable only from our own control panel. Messages are sent through Meta's official Instagram API. Both providers process the data on our behalf only.

How long we keep it

We keep your conversation while it is useful for answering you. You can ask us to delete it at any time, as described below.

Deleting your data

Send our Instagram account a message saying delete my data. We will delete everything we hold about you within 30 days and confirm in the same chat. Full steps: Data deletion instructions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
